Privacy Policy

Recetario de la Abuela · Last updated November 5, 2026

Recetario de la Abuela does not collect, store or share any personal information.

What stays on your phone

The recipes you save, the ingredients and steps you check off, your servings and measurement choices, the ingredients you enter in ¿Qué tengo? and your appearance setting are stored only on your device. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes them.

What we don't do

The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. It does not use your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone. All recipes are included in the app, and it works without an internet connection.

Sharing a recipe

If you use the share button, the recipe text goes only where you choose to send it, through your phone's share sheet.

Children

The app collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions? Write to the developer through the support page listed in the App Store.
