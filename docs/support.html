Recetario de la Abuela · Support

Traditional Mexican home cooking as lotería cards, with pressure-cooker versions, servings for any size of crowd, and a search for what's in your kitchen.

How do I change how many people a recipe makes?

Open a recipe and use ¿Para cuántos?: the − and + buttons, or Mitad, Original and Doble. Every ingredient amount adjusts. Amounts written inside the steps and cooking times stay as written.

Can I see grams instead of cups?

Yes. In ¿Para cuántos?, switch Medidas to Gramos · l. The choice applies to every recipe.

How does ¿Qué tengo? work?

Type what you have, separated by commas, in English or Spanish, or tap the quick picks. Herbs and spices only count if you add them. Turn off "I have the basics" if you're out of salt, oil, lard, sugar or broth.

The screen keeps turning off while I cook

Tap the olla (cook mode) at the top of a recipe. The text gets bigger and the screen stays on while that recipe is open.

Light or dark

Tap the half-circle button at the top of Recetas, ¿Qué tengo? or Despensa and choose Like the phone, Light or Dark.

Contact

Found a mistake in a recipe, or have a family version to share? Open an issue on the project's GitHub page, or reply through the App Store listing.
