## Description (4,000)

Recetario de la Abuela is a cookbook of traditional Mexican home cooking, region by region, the way it's made in Mexican kitchens and not the Americanized version.

Every recipe is a lotería card. Pozole rojo from Jalisco, cochinita pibil from Yucatán, carnitas from Quiroga, aguachile from Sinaloa, pescado a la veracruzana, mole poblano, birria, tamales, chilaquiles, conchas, café de olla and more, each with its story, its region and the cantada the lotería caller would sing.

TRADICIONAL, OLLA EXPRÉS Y VARIANTES
• The traditional method, plus a pressure-cooker version where it makes sense
• Variations under each dish: pozole verde and blanco, birria estilo Tijuana, caldo siete mares, pescado zarandeado, flan napolitano, tamales de rajas and de dulce
• Secretos de la abuela: the tips that make the difference

¿PARA CUÁNTOS?
• Cook for more or fewer people, or cut a recipe in half: every ingredient amount adjusts, and so do the amounts in the steps
• Cups and pounds or grams and liters, your choice

¿QUÉ TENGO?
• Type what's in your kitchen, in English or Spanish, and see what you can cook
• Typos and plurals are fine; herbs and spices are optional
• See exactly what's missing: "Te falta: hojas de maíz"

EN LA COCINA
• Check off ingredients and steps as you go
• Cook mode: bigger text, and the screen stays on
• Save favorites with the milagro heart
• A pantry guide to chiles, with heat levels and how to toast and soak them
• Hoy hay… follows the calendar: jueves pozolero, Sunday barbacoa, tamales for la Candelaria, pan de muerto for the ofrenda

Everything works offline. No account, no ads, no tracking.

## App Privacy

Answer **Data Not Collected**. The app stores favorites, checkmarks and settings only on the device, and sends nothing anywhere.

## Age rating

Most answers are None. The recipes mention beer, pulque and mezcal as ingredients or drinks served alongside, so answer **Alcohol, Tobacco, or Drug Use or References: Infrequent/Mild**. Apple then sets the rating.

## Notes for App Review

No sign-in is required. All content is bundled in the app and works offline. To try the main features: open any card in Recetas; use ¿Para cuántos? to change servings; switch Medidas to Gramos; open the ¿Qué tengo? tab and tap "puerco, chiles secos, maíz pozolero".
